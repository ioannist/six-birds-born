# Full rank-two census amendment (2026-10-08)

The census population is the whole rank-two cohort, rather than the subset
whose final matrices the historical run happened to retain. Construction
completion is separate from the registered robust-threshold outcome; missing
inputs and invalid certificates still block completion. The amended bound and
complete population are specified below.

This amendment supplements, and does not replace, the registered
`born_census_structural_protocol_v1` in `census.json` (including its registered
3/20 check). The historical 120-case certificates, specification identity and
3/20 successes remain historical evidence.

The population is every rank-two context in the fixed 11,177-context closed
collection: 2,122 contexts, in the existing SHA-256 complete-provenance-key order.
Exponent-one matrices, source spans and immutable raw/package joins are required
for completeness; missing evidence blocks completion instead of selecting a
smaller cohort. No performance or structural-success filtering is permitted.
The historical 120 were the subset whose final matrices were retained; the
full-capture run recovered the full cohort.

Completion/execution and threshold outcomes are separate. A valid completed
construction remains completed when its maximal gap falls short of registered
3/20. Summaries and seals must record all outcomes; missing inputs, invalid
certificates and incomplete construction still block. The retrospective bound
is 1/25, with the exact-arithmetic minimum enclosure
[0.0438357, 0.0438358]. Report registered 3/20 separately (2,005 successes,
117 shortfalls), never reinterpret those shortfalls as successes at 3/20.

Lexicographic witnesses use (I(c), J(c)), with all earlier i<j pairs rejected.
An active atom is singular exactly when w is constant on its block. All singular
effects must be checked nonzero and nonprojective; singleton size is diagnostic.

Preserve both worked fixtures by identity. Historical census case 0 is full-run
index 1284; it is not the earliest full-cohort case. The other worked fixture's
own registered identity stays fixed.

Full-cohort Lean certification is required separately. NOT_APPLICABLE does not
satisfy this full-cohort decision. Lean now certifies exhaustive maximization
with lexicographic ties, the uniform 1/25 bound and the minimum interval, using
the full-cohort Census FullInputs, FullWitness and AuditFull modules. Source-record acquisition and
complete-record binding remain external; Python checks the retained inputs and
exact certificate fields.

Resume may apply this amendment to earlier completed construction artifacts
only after hash verification and checking their completion certificates. Append
reclassification receipts and new summaries; preserve old attempts, statuses,
plans and summaries. Earlier runs and units need not be executed again.
