## 4. Frozen models and parameter accounting

For each eligible context and $r\in\{2,3,4\}\cap\{r<m\}$, fit the record square

\[
 z_0=U^\top\sqrt u,\quad z_t=A^t z_0,\quad
 B_t(j)=\frac{(Uz_t)_j^2}{\|Uz_t\|_2^2},\quad U^\top U=I_r,\ A\in\mathbb R^{r\times r}.
\]

Use $t=0$ only to form $z_0$, never to score an artificial reconstruction of $u$. Normalize $A$ to Frobenius norm one after each update; if $\|Uz_t\|<10^{-12}$, output uniform (1/m), record the event, and call a test run invalid if it occurs in a scored cell. Fitting minimizes mean training trajectory log loss at $t=1:8$, with probability floor $10^{-12}$ used **only in the loss**. No micro-informed initialization. The implemented effective count is $mr-1$: Stiefel $U$ plus $A$, quotient the shared $O(r)$ gauge and the positive global scale of $A$ for positive times. Publish the raw $mr-r(r+1)/2+r^2$ count too; use the larger raw count as the **fairness cap** $C_r$, so no disputed quotient gives the square a budget advantage.

## 5. Primary score, inference, and decision

For each $c\in\mathcal E_r$, define $\Delta_{c,r}=\operatorname{excess}(\text{validation-selected capped classical})-\operatorname{excess}(B)$ and $\Gamma_{c,r}=\operatorname{excess}(\text{optimized diagonal})-\operatorname{excess}(B)$. The **six primary estimands** are the equal-context means $\bar\Delta_r=|\mathcal E_r|^{-1}\sum_{c\in\mathcal E_r}\Delta_{c,r}$ and $\bar\Gamma_r=|\mathcal E_r|^{-1}\sum_{c\in\mathcal E_r}\Gamma_{c,r}$ for $r=2,3,4$. **Margins:** On training blocks 910001–910010 make five disjoint two-block evaluation folds. For each fold fit the complete selector on the other eight blocks using training recipes, evaluate the held-out two blocks, and compute the equal-context rank mean $\bar\Delta_r^{(f)}$ on the frozen $\mathcal E_r$. Set $\delta_r=\max\{0.01,2\,\operatorname{SD}_{f=1}^5(\bar\Delta_r^{(f)})\}$ nat per outcome. The cross-term margin is 0.01 nat; the order and same-rank nonnegative margins are $\delta_r$. Publish folds and margins before test opening. Training-only fold results, not locked-test variation, can raise the 0.01 floor.

**Exact paired inference algorithm.** For contrast $j$ and locked-test block $b=1{:}20$, let $g^{(j)}_{c,a,b,\ell,t}=[-\log \tilde p_{\rm rival}(Y_{c,a,b,\ell,t})]-[-\log \tilde p_{\rm square}(Y_{c,a,b,\ell,t})]$, where $\tilde p$ is the already specified floor-and-renormalize scoring probability. Both terms use the **same observed label** and sealed predictions. For $\Delta$ use the capped classical rival; for $\Gamma$ the optimized diagonal; for $\Omega$ the selected temporal rival. Define the block statistic and estimator, with $N_r=|\mathcal E_r|$:

\[
 x_{b,j}=\frac1{N_r}\sum_{c\in\mathcal E_r}\frac1{12}\sum_{a=48}^{59}\frac1{32\cdot8}\sum_{\ell=1}^{32}\sum_{t=1}^{8}g^{(j)}_{c,a,b,\ell,t},\qquad
 \hat\theta_j=\frac1{20}\sum_{b=1}^{20}x_{b,j}.
\]

The audit-oracle subtraction cancels in each paired difference. For reversal $R$ set $g=\ell_{\rm square}-\ell_{\rm same\ rank\ ED}$; an upper bound for $R$ is obtained by applying the lower-bound algorithm to $-x_{b,R}$ and negating its result. Use exactly **200,000** bootstrap draws of 20 block indices with replacement from $\{1,\ldots,20\}$, generated by this exact counter rule: for draw $k=0{:}199999$, position $i=0{:}19$ and retry counter $z=0,1,\ldots$, hash UTF-8 ASCII `TestB/v6/bootstrap/{frozen_spec_sha256}/{family_id}/{k}/{i}/{z}` with SHA-256; read its first eight bytes as little-endian unsigned $x$, retry while $x\ge\lfloor2^{64}/20\rfloor20$, and otherwise take block index $1+(x\bmod20)$. Use family IDs `primary`, `reversal`, and `order`. The same index matrix is used jointly for every contrast/rank in a family; $\hat\theta^*_{k,j}$ is the mean of the 20 resampled $x_{b,j}$. For $H_{0,j}(d):\theta_j\le d$, define the centered one-sided p-value

\[
 p_j(d)=\frac{1+\#\{k\in[200000]:\hat\theta^*_{k,j}-\hat\theta_j\ge\hat\theta_j-d\}}{200001}.
\]

Use IEEE-754 binary64 values in the displayed operation order; **equality counts in the tail against rejection**. At any fixed threshold vector, sort raw p-values ascending, breaking exact ties by immutable slot ID `D2,G2,D3,G3,D4,G4` for the primary family (`R2,R3,R4` and `O2,O3,O4` for the three-test families). Holm step-down rejects sorted slot $k$ iff every slot $i\le k$ has $p_{(i)}\le0.05/(M-i+1)$, where $M=6$ or $3$; stop at the first failure. Report Holm-adjusted $p_{(k)}=\min\{1,\max_{i\le k}(M-i+1)p_{(i)}\}$ at the frozen margins. Empty-rank slots have $p=1$ and no finite bound.

For **nominal simultaneous rectangular one-sided lower bounds under bootstrap validity**, invert the worst-case first Holm critical value $\alpha/M$: $L_j=\sup\{d:p_j(d)\le0.05/M\}$. This is the coordinate projection of the Holm closed-test family when the other thresholds are unrestricted; it is deliberately conservative. These are **nominal simultaneous bounds under bootstrap validity**: Bonferroni controls multiplicity if the marginal bootstrap p-values are valid, but 20 blocks do not guarantee finite-sample coverage. It is the bound used for pass gates even if Holm's adjusted p-value at a margin is smaller. Search separately for each nonempty slot with the **same frozen bootstrap draws**: start at $\hat\theta_j\pm1$ nat, double the bracket width until the lower endpoint rejects and upper endpoint does not, then bisect until width $<10^{-8}$ nat; report the rejecting lower endpoint, rounded downward to $10^{-8}$ nat. Because $p_j(d)$ is nondecreasing, this uniquely specifies the bound and tie behavior. For three-test lower families use $M=3$; for the three-test reversal upper family apply the same $M=3$ algorithm to $-R$. The p-value floor $1/200001$ resolves the smallest Holm threshold $0.05/6$. Do not resample individual readings as independent units.

**Separate claim gates:** Let $N_{c,r}$ be the fitted **same-rank** nonnegative transition, whether or not it exceeds the cap. Define the reversal $R_r$ as the equal-context mean of $\operatorname{excess}(B)-\operatorname{excess}(N_{c,r})$. Obtain nominal simultaneous 95% **upper** bounds under bootstrap validity for $R_2,R_3,R_4$ by the same whole-block bootstrap with Holm over those three tests. The no-reversal gate for rank $r$ requires its upper bound $<\delta_r$: the same-rank positive rival is not better by the practical margin. Define $\Omega_r$ as the equal-context mean of $\operatorname{excess}(\text{validation-selected temporal rival})-\operatorname{excess}(B)$; obtain nominal simultaneous 95% **lower** bounds under bootstrap validity by Holm over these three order tests. The order gate requires its lower bound $>\delta_r$. These extra gates can only restrict a pass, so the six-test primary family remains the declared discovery family. Publish both secondary families and bounds even if the primary test fails.

**Sealed audit-only signal check:** After all predictions and fits are sealed, use the exact audit marginal laws to form, for each context, the best time- and preparation-independent constant $q_c^*=\frac1{12\cdot8}\sum_{a=48}^{59}\sum_{t=1}^{8}p^*_{c,a,t}$. Its oracle mean gain is $S_r=\frac1{N_r}\sum_{c\in\mathcal E_r}\frac1{12\cdot8}\sum_{a=48}^{59}\sum_{t=1}^{8}D_{\rm KL}(p^*_{c,a,t}\|q_c^*)$. This is the maximum expected log-loss improvement any predictor could obtain over the **best context-specific pooled constant** on the named test preparation/time distribution. Publish $q_c^*$, each context's gain and $S_r$ only after sealing; none may enter fitting, validation, model selection or seed derivation. If a scope-passing rank has $S_r\le\delta_r$, its available oracle signal **cannot reach** the frozen order margin: report that rank as **uninformative at its selected lag**, not as a square failure or pass. Do not remove its contexts or recompute other rank means.

A **pass** requires some rank with a nonempty $\mathcal E_r$, locked-test scope passed, $S_r>\delta_r$, both primary bounds over margins, no-reversal passed, order passed, and all validity controls passed. The claim is: **the learned record square beats the capped classical family and optimized diagonal on the equal-context mean, is noninferior within margin to the same-rank nonnegative transition, and uses ordered readings, approximately closed on the named preparation/time distribution.** A failed no-reversal or order gate prevents this pass; report which gate failed. The sealed pseudo-campaign power audit in Section 6 must use these exact statistics, bounds, six-test Holm family and scope rule; fewer than 80/100 passing pseudo-campaigns leaves Test B uncertified here before real test opening. Separately, use the ten training blocks only to form 1,000 synthetic 20-block campaigns by whole-block resampling of the five leave-two-out evaluation vectors, and publish estimated rejection rates at the observed training effects and at the frozen margins; this audit is descriptive and cannot change the design. No post-test seed extension or test-based retry is allowed.
