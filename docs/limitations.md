# Limitations

## Construction, reproduction and evidence

The repository constructs a real rank-two carrier, trace readouts, principal-root
density instruments and finite non-adaptive program semantics. Imported effects,
external density preparations and measurement updates are declared analytic
operations. Their algebraic validity does not show that the stochastic substrate
produces them or physically realizes measurement. Native kernel transport and
the adopted instrument have separate interfaces and claims.

A fresh clone can run the **full public reproduction pipeline**, including
new captures, units, census certificates, worked cases, performance attachments,
population inference, full-cohort Lean inputs/build/audit, Test B production and
locked scoring. It needs the pinned toolchains, dependencies, time, storage and
memory described in the [README](../README.md#quick-start). The full pipeline
completed with 16/16 regenerated claims VERIFIED. The completed archive
comparison returned protocol PASS and reported results reproduced PASS across
18 stages (worked: PASS_WITH_DECLARED_LIMITS): **958,438,725** values compared,
all exact, maximum absolute difference **0**. Its paper checks cover **358 result
quantities**, with **1,477** exact comparisons at full precision, in displayed
form and against manuscript definitions; **45** program settings/constants are
recorded as settings, none unavailable. Figure coordinates and literal labels
are not compared. The 120 historical census cases are compared directly;
2,002 added cases, 4,004 added attachment results, 4,314 added Lean declarations
and 13 historical-case outputs without archived counterparts are validated
independently, with no historical-match credit. Named storage/process
projections and documentary dispositions delimit this agreement; it does not
certify the historical executable or its correspondence with the public source.
The worked stage retains its method limits and unresolved generated-H boundary.

The reduced smoke profile completes without a Lean build and records
**REDUCED_NONCONFIRMATORY** checks, formal **NOT_APPLICABLE**, and no verified
full-cohort claims. Its Test B OUT_OF_ELIGIBLE_SCOPE outcome is expected. Smoke
exercises implementation paths; it cannot certify the registered experiments.

Verification commands read existing evidence; they do not perform new sampling,
fitting, model selection or confirmation. The 14 no-data verifications need the
compiled public Lean project except for their explicitly Python-only controls.
Historical experimental comparison additionally requires retained reference data,
its manifest, ledger/aliases and retained historical Lean source tree/audit,
supplied separately. The current manuscript is shipped as `paper/main.tex`;
an earlier revision is needed separately only for a comparison targeting it.
A clone includes producers and reference-format examples, not those large
archives or private paper-preparation files. Full-cohort census IDs accept the
completed 2,122-case stage outputs or full regenerated bundle; the older
120-case bundle is not a substitute. A hash-bound attachment establishes identity,
not the calculation that originally produced it. Unit-law recomputation and
population inference are separate from retention checks in the
[claim table](claims.md).

## Formal and numerical scope

Lean covers the declared real 2×2 density/effect domains, trace valuation,
representation obstructions, complete finite Kraus programs, frame transfer,
the product/sequential comparator bridge, visible-event descent, package
projection laws and the executable principal-root formula. General carrier/trace,
single-test finite-state, encoder-image/non-affinity, inherited non-density
assignment and fibre/lift results are machine-checked with their stated premises.
Python still checks implementation coefficient parity, descriptor parsing,
protocol records and serialization.

The census is **all 2,122 rank-two contexts** in the fixed 11,177-context
collection. Lean's full census packages certify generalized (I(c), J(c))
strictness witnesses, maximal raw-label-zero trace gaps with three-map label
eligibility and lexicographic ties, the retrospective uniform **1/25** bound,
and the minimum enclosure **[0.0438357, 0.0438358]** with the upper witness at
case 425. The registered **3/20** result remains **2,005 pass / 117 shortfalls**;
a shortfall is an outcome, separate from construction completion. I(c) is
nonzero in 25 contexts.

There are **17,008 active atoms**, **16,693 positive** and **315 singular** in
**272 contexts**. Singularity means w is constant on the active block; this
includes **283 singleton** and **32 nonsingleton** atoms. Every active singular
effect is nonzero and nonprojective. The full Lean package has 266 shards,
FullInputs, FullWitness and AuditFull. The historical 120-case endpoints remain
available as retained history. Full-profile certification requires full-cohort
coverage; formal NOT_APPLICABLE is insufficient.

The original B04 retained kernel, four exact K/K² fibre witnesses, root
bounds and density/joined-state no-factor results are proved. B05 certifies
normalized ray ensembles with equal density barycenters and initial lens reads
but unequal registered **completion-apparatus** word probabilities; this
apparatus is separate from the adopted principal-root instrument. B06 proves
finite-fragment non-density existence and exclusion of every density
representation. Polytope characterization and route-independence method
qualifications may remain retained rather than reproduced, and are named in
the comparison report. **Extension/classification on generated H remains
unresolved**, including when comparison completes with declared limits.

Archive acquisition, execution identity, complete-record/source-export binding,
cryptographic provenance and conditional floating arithmetic premises remain
external to Lean. Python checks commitments, joins and stated numerical
certificate obligations; it does not prove historical retention integrity or
unconditional platform accuracy. `reproduce replay-maps` recomputes stationary
estimates, emitted vectors and all three label maps from the retained exponent-one
K on all 2,122 cases and the original worked case. Exact replay of retained
records does not certify the execution that originally produced each matrix.

No formal result establishes empirical significance, acquisition cost, native
formation, useful return, promotion or quantum specificity. Full-real-effect
valuation classification does not classify the generated restricted fragment.
The obstructions assume their stated response/mixture conditions. An unrestricted
access-matched stochastic interpreter reproduces the admitted program laws.

NumPy is pinned because literal constructor reproduction is environment-sensitive;
a valid projector bound alone does not certify the executed prediction pipeline.
Original implementation outcomes remain separate from completed certificates:
overall 1,115 pass / 1,007 fail, coordinate 2,078 / 44, symbolic 1,141 / 981,
and bit equality 27 / 2,095 in the full cohort. Recorded native exception events
and conditional arithmetic qualifications remain explicit.

The apparatus validates the centered frame, exact squared norm and complete
lens dimensions. Command admission checks positive effects summing to I2 and
the principal-root rule, including under Python optimization. Finite numerical
programs certify positive branch support. If finite enclosures cannot resolve
support, they return **UNRESOLVED_NUMERICAL**; the ideal-real program theorem
still applies to the mathematical program. Numerical refusal does not establish
zero mass or an invalid instrument.

## Test B

Historical TB01 verification reconstructs the recorded learned-record-square
failure at ranks 2–4 on the named preparation/time distribution, through
production and independent score-only paths. It performs no refit or new test
opening. Public reproduction separately supplies synthetic certification,
training/validation sampling, fitting and pre-test prediction commitment, then
locked sampling/scoring only after those commitments. Its new result need not
have the historical negative verdict. Neither path makes a tick/arrow or
measurement-order claim; bootstrap bounds retain their nominal qualification.
